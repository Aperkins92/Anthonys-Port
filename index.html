THE SHORT VERSION

More than a career change.

I grew up in Yakima, Washington, with football being a huge part of my life. I played, watched, studied, and analyzed the game whenever I could.

Eventually, an injury changed the direction of playing football, but it did not take away my interest in the game. Instead, I found other ways to stay connected to it and to technology.

Years of stagehand work introduced me to concerts, sporting events, special effects, production crews, and technical environments. That experience eventually led to opportunities with JDL FX and SkyCam.

Now I am bringing everything I have learned from those environments into Information Technology.

Real-world experience taught me how to stay calm, communicate with a team, and figure things out when the plan changes.
