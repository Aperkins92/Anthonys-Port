Professional Experience Projects

Live Event Production & Stagehand Experience

Throughout my career in the live event industry, I worked as a stagehand supporting concerts, sporting events, NFL productions, and large-scale entertainment events. My responsibilities included assisting with event setups, equipment movement, organization, and working with production teams to complete projects under strict deadlines.

Working in live production taught me how important communication, teamwork, and preparation are. Every event required multiple departments to work together while maintaining safety and efficiency.

This experience helped me develop skills that transfer into many careers, including leadership, problem-solving, time management, and the ability to stay focused in high-pressure situations.

Skills Developed:

  • Teamwork and communication
  • Safety awareness
  • Problem-solving
  • Time management
  • Working under pressure

Personal Portfolio Website

This project is a multi-page portfolio website built using HTML and CSS. The purpose of this website is to showcase my background, skills, experience, and goals while learning how professional websites are created.

I created multiple pages including a Home page, Projects page, and About page. Each page uses semantic HTML elements such as headers, navigation menus, sections, articles, and footers to create an organized website structure.

Stagehand Safety & Efficiency Research Project

This research project focuses on challenges within the live event industry, specifically how stagehands balance workplace safety with the pressure to complete setups and breakdowns quickly.

The project explored how communication, planning, training, and time pressure can affect workplace safety. I researched ways organizations can improve safety practices while still maintaining efficiency.

This project connected my personal experience working in live events with academic research and helped me improve my writing, research, and critical thinking skills.

Skills Used:

  • Research and analysis
  • Professional writing
  • Critical thinking
  • Source evaluation
