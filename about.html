About Me

From Yakima to the stage, football fields, and IT.

My Background

I grew up in Yakima, Washington, and football was a major passion of mine from childhood.

I played grid kids football with friends and spent a lot of time watching and analyzing NFL games. My family was split between Packers and Seahawks fans, so football was always part of the conversation.

Although an injury from a dirtbike race broke my back and arm and sidelined me from playing football, it never took away my love of the game. Instead, it gave me even more time to watch, study, and analyze football.

Football

Football has been a major part of my life for years. Working around football production allowed me to experience the sport from a different perspective.

Stagehand Work

I have worked as a stagehand for many years.

Stagehand work has allowed me to travel and work on a huge variety of concerts, productions, sporting events, and special events.

The job requires teamwork, communication, physical preparation, troubleshooting, attention to detail, and the ability to adapt quickly.

Working Behind the Scenes

Live-event work means that a lot of what happens behind the scenes has to be completed correctly before the audience ever sees the finished production.

Live Events

I have helped build and prepare environments for concerts and events involving many different performers and productions.

Artists & Events

Throughout my stagehand career, I have helped set up concerts and events for artists and performers including:

Ginuwine Trace Adkins Sullivan King Excision KISS Tool Bert Kreischer Gabriel Iglesias And many others

Live Event Memories

Working live events has given me opportunities to meet people, travel, learn new skills, and experience productions from behind the scenes.

SkyCam Experience

My stagehand experience eventually led to opportunities working with SkyCam, the football camera system used to capture moving shots of football games.

Being able to work around football production connected two important parts of my life: my professional experience and my passion for football.

My IT Journey

I am now continuing to develop my information technology skills.

I am especially interested in learning through hands-on projects. Building this portfolio has helped me practice HTML, CSS, JavaScript, debugging, organization, and problem solving.

My goal is to continue learning, build useful projects, and keep improving my technical skills.
