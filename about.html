THE STORY BEHIND THE WORK

A different path into technology.

Football, production, special effects, live events, and now Information Technology.

WHERE IT STARTED

Football was always part of the story.

I grew up in Yakima, Washington, and football became a major part of my life from an early age.

I played grid kids football with friends and spent countless hours watching the NFL. Football was not just something I watched. I liked paying attention to what teams were doing and analyzing the game.

My family had its own football rivalry. Some were Packers fans while others followed the Seahawks. That made football an even bigger part of our lives.

Eventually, a dirtbike racing accident left me with a broken back and arm. That changed my ability to play football, but it did not change how much I cared about the sport.

ORIGIN Yakima, Washington Football • Competition • Curiosity
