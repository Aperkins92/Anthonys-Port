Who I Am

My name is Anthony, and I am a college student focused on building new skills, creating opportunities, and continuing to grow both personally and professionally. My background combines hands-on experience, creativity, teamwork, and a passion for learning new technology.

Before returning to school, I spent many years working in the live event industry as a stagehand. During that time, I helped build concerts, sporting events, NFL productions, and large-scale entertainment events. Working behind the scenes gave me experience with fast-paced environments where preparation, communication, and teamwork were essential.

My stagehand experience taught me valuable skills that continue to help me today. I learned how to solve problems quickly, stay organized under pressure, follow safety procedures, and work with different teams to complete projects successfully.

Every event required attention to detail and the ability to adapt when unexpected challenges happened. These experiences helped develop my leadership skills, reliability, and ability to contribute as part of a team.

I am currently studying Information Technology and developing skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, and website development. I am learning how technology can create useful solutions and improve the way people work, communicate, and interact with digital tools.

My goal is to combine my real-world experience with my growing IT knowledge to create projects that demonstrate creativity, technical ability, and problem-solving skills.
