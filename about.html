Who I Am

My name is Anthony, and I am a college student focused on building new skills and creating opportunities for my future. My background combines hands-on experience, creativity, and a passion for learning new technology.

Before returning to school, I spent many years working in the live event industry as a stagehand. I helped build concerts, sporting events, NFL productions, and large-scale entertainment events.

Working behind the scenes taught me the importance of teamwork, communication, preparation, and staying calm under pressure. Every event required planning, problem-solving, and working with different teams to accomplish a common goal.

My experience in event production helped me develop leadership skills, attention to detail, safety awareness, and the ability to adapt when challenges happen.

I am currently studying Information Technology and developing skills in HTML, CSS, responsive design, JavaScript, and website development. I enjoy learning how technology can solve problems and create useful solutions.

My goal is to combine my real-world experience with my growing IT knowledge to create projects that show creativity, teamwork, and technical ability.
