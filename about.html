About Me

From Yakima to the stage, football fields, and IT.

My Story

I was born in 1992 in Yakima, Washington. Growing up, I was always involved in something. I played football and basketball, and I started riding dirt bikes when I was seven years old. I was always looking for something to do, something to learn, or something I could get better at.

Football became a big part of my life when I was young. I played Grid Kids football with friends, including Cooper Kupp, and spent a lot of time playing football during recess. We would make up plays, run around the playground, and just have fun.

As I got older, my interest in football went beyond playing it. I started watching the NFL all the time and became interested in the strategy behind the game. I liked figuring out why certain plays worked and others did not.

My family made football even more interesting. My mom and oldest brother were Packers fans, while my grandpa and I were Seahawks fans. Watching games together usually meant arguing about plays, talking about players, and having fun with it.

Live Event Work

After school, I eventually found my way into live-event production. I have spent years working as a stagehand, helping build and tear down concerts and events.

Stagehand work is very hands-on. It takes teamwork, communication, attention to detail, and the ability to solve problems quickly.

I have worked around large productions and major venues. I have also worked football games, which allowed me to combine my interest in football with the production work I was already doing.

Some of My Interests and Experience

Football has been one of my biggest interests since I was young.

Sports are still a big part of what I enjoy.

Live events and production work have been a major part of my career.

I have met a lot of people and worked on many different productions through live events.

Why I Am Getting Into IT

After years of working as a stagehand and doing physical work, I have started thinking more about what I want to do long term.

That is one of the reasons I decided to jump into the world of IT. I have always liked figuring out how things work. I like troubleshooting problems, learning new things, and finding out why something is not working.

I am currently working through a two-year IT program and building skills in areas like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, cybersecurity, and technology.

I am still learning, and I definitely do not know everything yet. That is okay. I am here to learn, make mistakes, fix them, and keep getting better.

My First Major IT Project

This website is my first major project for school, but I am also building it for myself.

I wanted something I could actually use to show what I have learned instead of just completing an assignment and moving on to the next one.

Building this site has given me a chance to work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript while learning how to troubleshoot problems when things do not work the way I expect them to.

What I Have Learned Along the Way

Football, basketball, dirt bikes, stagehand work, traveling, JDL FX, SkyCam, and now IT are all very different things.

But they have all taught me some of the same things: pay attention, keep learning, work with people, figure out problems, and do not quit just because something gets difficult.

I am not completely sure where IT will take me yet, but I am excited to find out. This is the beginning of another chapter for me, and this website is one of the first things I am building along the way.
