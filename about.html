Who I Am

My name is Anthony, and I am a college student focused on building new skills, creating opportunities, and continuing to grow personally and professionally. My background combines hands-on experience, creativity, teamwork, and a passion for learning new technology.

Before returning to school, I spent many years working in the live event industry as a stagehand. I helped build concerts, sporting events, NFL productions, and large-scale entertainment events where teamwork, preparation, and safety were extremely important.

Working behind the scenes taught me how to communicate with different teams, solve problems quickly, manage time, and stay focused in high-pressure environments. Every event required planning, organization, and people working together toward a shared goal.

My experience in event production helped me develop leadership skills, attention to detail, reliability, and the ability to adapt when unexpected challenges happened. These skills continue to help me as I learn technology and work on new projects.

I am currently studying Information Technology and developing skills in HTML, CSS, responsive design, JavaScript, and website development. I am learning how technology can create useful solutions and improve the way people work, communicate, and interact with digital tools.

My goal is to combine my real-world experience with my growing IT knowledge to create projects that demonstrate creativity, teamwork, problem-solving, and technical ability.
