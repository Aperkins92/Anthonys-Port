Who I Am

My name is Anthony, and I am a college student focused on building new skills and creating opportunities for my future. Before returning to school, I spent many years working in the live event industry as a stagehand supporting concerts, sporting events, and large-scale productions.

Working behind the scenes taught me the importance of teamwork, communication, preparation, and staying calm under pressure. Live events require people from many different areas to work together, and I learned how every role contributes to the success of a project.

My experience in event production helped me develop a strong work ethic, leadership skills, and the ability to solve problems quickly. Whether setting up equipment, organizing tasks, or working with a crew to meet deadlines, I learned the value of reliability and dedication.

I am currently continuing my education and developing skills in technology, web development, writing, and critical thinking. Creating websites has allowed me to combine my creativity with technical skills while learning how digital tools can be used to solve problems.

Outside of school and work, I enjoy drawing, listening to music, following sports, gaming, and continuing to improve my creativity. These interests help me stay motivated and continue learning new skills.

My goal is to combine my real-world experience with the technical knowledge I am gaining through education. I want to continue growing, take on new challenges, and create projects that show my skills and experiences.
